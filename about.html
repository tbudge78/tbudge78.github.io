About us

We grew up in Troutdale, Oregon — a small suburb east of Portland, at the mouth of the Columbia River Gorge — as members of the Troutdale Ward of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

What to expect

Stories about awkwardness, camp-outs, regret, triumph, and way too many unrequited crushes. Some funny, some sentimental, all true (as best we remember them).

This is an independent, personal project. The opinions and memories shared are our own and do not represent the official position of the Church.
