Our story

Why “Troutdale Ward Picnic”?

Troutdale, Oregon sits where the Sandy River meets the Columbia — a small town of tall firs, mossy rocks, and one very well-known park. For a kid in the Troutdale Ward, the ward picnic was the high point of the summer: a potluck on the grass, a baseball game, and every familiar face in one place.

Bryan and I spent our whole childhood in this community and were wondering how we could preserve some of the memories. This podcast records an oral history of the people that made our childhood so great and the events that shaped our character, the good stuff and the bad stuff. So, if you are curious, have a listen to the Troutdale Ward Picnic.

Why Exit 17?

We talk quite a lot about Exit 17. That is the number of the exit on I-84 where you can either leave or enter Troutdale. This was our passage way to the world and the welcome sign for when we come home. When we talk of Exit 17, it means that all the good things we take with us in the world and all the good things we bring back from our adventures. All of those have a part in this in making Troutdale our home.
