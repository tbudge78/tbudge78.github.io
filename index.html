A podcast from Exit 17

Growing up in Troutdale, Oregon, as a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints meant Sunday meetings, ward activities, potlucks, youth dances — and the summer picnic where the whole ward showed up. Troutdale Ward Picnic is a podcast of stories, memories, and laughs from that small town and the people who made it home.

Why “Troutdale Ward Picnic”?

Troutdale, Oregon sits where the Sandy River meets the Columbia — a small town of tall firs, mossy rocks, and one very well-known park. For a kid in the Troutdale Ward, the ward picnic was the high point of the summer: a potluck on the grass, a softball game, and every familiar face in one place.
